Short vertical highlights from live streams

GalbaClips is a private tool operated by GalbaClipper. It watches the Twitch streams of creators who have given permission (directly or through an official clipping campaign), detects the moments where the audience reacts the most, and turns them into short vertical videos.

How it works

  1. Highlights are detected from the public chat activity and the stream audio.
  2. Each clip is cropped to a vertical format, captioned, and credited to the original creator.
  3. The owner reviews the clips in a private dashboard, chooses the visibility and settings for each post, and approves them.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X accounts through the official APIs.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The tool is not offered to the public and does not post on anyone else's behalf.
