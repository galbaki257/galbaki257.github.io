Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

GalbaClips ("the App") is a personal tool operated by GalbaClipper ("we") to publish short highlight clips to our own social media accounts. The App is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • YouTube API Services: an OAuth token for our own channel, the IDs of videos we upload, and their statistics (views, likes, comments, average view percentage, estimated revenue).
  • TikTok: an OAuth token for our own account, our display name, the privacy options available to our account, and statistics of videos we posted.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): a Page access token for our own Page, the ID of the linked Instagram professional account, and insights of Reels we posted.
  • X: an OAuth token for our own account and public metrics of posts we published.
  • Twitch: public stream status and public chat messages of channels we are permitted to clip. Chat messages are processed in memory to detect highlights; only short anonymous excerpts are kept with each clip's metadata and are deleted with the clip.

How we use it

Only to upload videos to our own accounts and to measure how they perform. Every video is selected and reviewed in our private dashboard.

  • TikTok: nothing is sent to TikTok until we confirm the specific videos to post, one by one or several at once after watching their previews: we see the destination account, choose the privacy level ourselves (no default), choose whether to allow comments, duets and stitches (none enabled by default), disclose commercial content when applicable, and accept TikTok's declarations. Confirmed videos are then posted at scheduled times.
  • YouTube: uploads go only to our own channel, either approved one by one or scheduled from clips we selected.

Videos made for paid clipping campaigns are disclosed as paid partnerships or branded content on each platform. The App does not add its own name, logo or watermark to videos. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising.

Storage and retention

All data is stored on our own machine or server and is never shared. Statistics and analytics of our own videos are kept only as long as needed to compare their performance; they are refreshed every few hours, and our authorization is re-verified at least every 30 days. Other API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Clips that are not selected are deleted the same day.

Third-party services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok, Meta, X and Twitch data is subject to those companies' own terms and policies.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions (or Google security settings)
  • TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites / Business Integrations
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions

Access can also be revoked from inside the App (clipper auth revoke youtube or tiktok): the token is revoked with the platform right away. When access is revoked, all API data obtained through it (statistics, revenue) is deleted: within 7 days when revoked from the App, and within 30 days when revoked from the platform's settings.

Data deletion: to request deletion of any data related to you, email galbaclipfarmer67@gmail.com; we delete it within 7 days.
